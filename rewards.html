Reward Calculation Methodology

Critical Smart Contract Vulnerabilities

For critical smart contract bugs, the reward amount is 10% of the funds directly at risk, up to a maximum of $250,000 USD. The calculation is based on the value of funds at risk at the time the bug report is submitted. A minimum reward of $50,000 is guaranteed to incentivize disclosure of critical vulnerabilities.

Example: If a vulnerability puts $800,000 worth of LBTC at risk, the reward would be $80,000. If $3,000,000 is at risk, the reward is capped at $250,000.

High Severity — Yield/Royalty Theft

High vulnerabilities involving theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield or royalties are rewarded between $10,000 and $50,000, depending on the funds at risk and capped at the maximum high reward.

High Severity — Temporary Freezing

For temporary fund freezing, the reward doubles from the full frozen value for every additional 24 hours that funds are frozen, up to the high reward cap. This incentivizes disclosure of freezing vulnerabilities proportional to their potential duration.

Repeatable Attack Limitations

  • For upgradeable/pausable contracts: only the initial attack is considered. The protocol can mitigate further exploitation by upgrading or pausing.
  • For non-upgradeable, non-pausable contracts: cumulative impact of repeatable attacks is considered, capped at 10% of total drained funds.
Payment Process

Step 1 — Submit Your Report

Submit through the Immunefi dashboard with a working proof-of-concept. Reports without PoC will not be triaged. Include affected contract addresses and a clear description of the impact.

Step 2 — Triage & Validation (up to 5 business days)

Immunefi triages the report and forwards it to the Lombard team. The team validates the vulnerability, reproduces the PoC, and determines severity based on the Immunefi Vulnerability Severity Classification System V2.3.

Step 3 — KYC Verification

Once the vulnerability is confirmed, KYC information is collected. Required: full name, date of birth, proof of address (redacted bank statement or utility bill), and government-issued ID or passport copy.

Step 4 — Payment in USDC

Payouts are handled by the Lombard team and denominated in USD, paid in USDC on Ethereum. The amount is calculated based on the average price between CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko at submission time. No adjustments for liquidity availability.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I receive payment in cryptocurrency other than USDC?
No. All rewards are paid exclusively in USDC on Ethereum. The USD value is locked at the time of report submission based on the average price from CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko.
What is the minimum reward I can receive?
The minimum reward for a critical smart contract vulnerability is $50,000. Medium severity is flat $5,000 and Low severity is flat $2,000. There is no minimum for High severity — it scales from $10,000 to $50,000 based on funds at risk.
How long does the payment process take?
After the vulnerability is confirmed and KYC is completed, the Lombard team processes payment within 30 days. Initial triage by Immunefi typically takes up to 5 business days. Complex or disputed cases may take longer.
What happens if someone else reports the same bug?
Only the first valid report of a given vulnerability is eligible for a reward. Duplicate reports submitted after the first valid submission will not be rewarded. Immunefi uses the timestamp of the first valid submission to determine priority.
Is there a reward for reporting web or app vulnerabilities?
Yes. Critical web/app bugs that lead to a loss of funds without user interaction or to private key leakage are rewarded $30,000. Other critical web/app impacts receive $15,000. Lower severity web/app bugs follow the standard severity table.
Do I need to provide a complete fix to receive the reward?
No. You are only required to provide a proof-of-concept demonstrating the vulnerability. You do not need to provide a fix. However, suggesting a fix is appreciated and may be taken into account during evaluation.
