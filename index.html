Information

Lombard is on a mission to unlock Bitcoin's potential as a dynamic financial tool by connecting it to DeFi with LBTC. LBTC is a secure Bitcoin Liquid Staking Token (LST), developed by Lombard on top of Babylon. It is a yield-bearing, natively cross-chain, liquid Bitcoin backed 1:1 by BTC.

Founded in April 2024, Lombard is dedicated to unlocking Bitcoin's potential as a dynamic financial tool. Lombard is building the universal standard for Bitcoin — secured by Bitcoin-aligned ecosystem players. The platform enables yield-bearing BTC to move cross-chain without fragmenting liquidity, paving the way for onboarding net new capital into DeFi.

With LBTC, Bitcoin can be held as a store of value and simultaneously used to lend, borrow, stake, trade, and transfer in DeFi across multiple blockchain ecosystems. This opens up over $1.4 trillion in Bitcoin capital for DeFi protocols.

Lombard is currently live on Ethereum mainnet in Public Beta. Eligible participants can stake native BTC and mint LBTC. For more information, please visit lombard.finance.

Smart Contracts — In Scope

The following smart contract categories are in scope for this program:

  • LBTC Token Contract — ERC-20 yield-bearing Bitcoin token on Ethereum
  • LBTC Minting & Redemption — Contracts managing BTC staking and LBTC issuance
  • Cross-chain Bridge Contracts — Lombard bridge infrastructure for multi-chain LBTC
  • Staking Pool Contracts — Babylon integration and stake management
  • Governance & Access Control — Admin roles, timelocks, multisig contracts
  • Fee & Treasury Contracts — Protocol fee distribution logic

For a full list of deployed contract addresses and detailed scope, see Smart Contracts and Mainnet Scope.

Program Rules

Primacy of Impact vs Primacy of Rules

Lombard adheres to the Primacy of Impact for Smart Contract Critical and High severity bugs. This means that the impact is prioritized rather than a specific asset — security researchers are encouraged to report all bugs with an in-scope impact, even if the affected assets are not explicitly listed in scope.

All other impacts are considered under Primacy of Rules, meaning they are bound by the terms and conditions set within this program.

Proof of Concept Required

A PoC demonstrating the bug's impact is required for all severities. Reports without a valid PoC will not be accepted. The PoC must comply with the Immunefi PoC Guidelines.

KYC Requirement

KYC information is required for payout processing. Required documents: full name, date of birth, proof of address, and a copy of a government-issued ID or passport.

Responsible Disclosure

All vulnerabilities must be reported privately through the Immunefi platform. Public disclosure before a fix is deployed is strictly prohibited and may result in disqualification from the reward. Lombard commits to triaging and responding to all reports within 5 business days.

Duplicate Reports

Only the first report of a given vulnerability will be eligible for a reward. Duplicate reports referencing the same issue will not be rewarded. Researchers are encouraged to be thorough and include as much detail as possible to ensure the bug is uniquely identified.

Non-Exploitable Reports

Theoretical vulnerabilities without a working proof of concept demonstrating real impact will not be rewarded. Reports must clearly show exploitability in practice, not just in theory.

Scope Limitations

Bug reports must relate to issues within the defined scope of this program. Reports outside of scope, including third-party dependencies and infrastructure not controlled by Lombard, are not eligible for rewards.
