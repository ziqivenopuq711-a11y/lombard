Mainnet Scope

All production contracts and assets deployed on mainnet networks that are in scope for the Lombard Bug Bounty program. Vulnerabilities affecting these assets are eligible for rewards up to $250,000.

Out of Scope — Mainnet

The following are explicitly out of scope for the mainnet bug bounty program:

  • All issues covered by previous audit reports (see audit folder)
  • Bugs reported in prior bug bounty rounds or audit competitions
  • Babylon protocol internals and Bitcoin consensus layer issues
  • Third-party DeFi integrations (Aave, Curve, etc.) not deployed by Lombard
  • Economic attacks requiring over $1M in capital to execute
  • Issues in testnet or staging deployments (covered under Testnet page)
  • Front-end vulnerabilities unless they lead to direct loss of funds
  • Gas optimization suggestions
